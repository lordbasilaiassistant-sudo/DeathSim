# Near-death experiences (NDEs)

Near-death experiences are **real as reports** — large numbers of people who survive cardiac arrest or other close brushes with death describe vivid, structured experiences. What is `[OPEN]` is their *cause* and what, if anything, they tell us about consciousness at death.

## What is well-documented `[SUPPORTED]`

A recurring, cross-culturally common (though not universal) **phenomenology** is consistently reported:

- A sense of peace or painlessness
- Out-of-body sensation (perceiving from above the body)
- Movement through a tunnel or darkness toward light
- A "life review"
- Encounters with deceased people or a presence
- A sense of a boundary or "point of no return"

That people *report* these things is not in doubt — it's documented across decades of interviews and prospective studies. The **AWARE-II** study (Parnia et al., 2023, *Resuscitation*), a 25-site prospective study of in-hospital cardiac arrest, found, among survivors: ~40% reported some perception of consciousness without explicit recall, ~20% reported features consistent with a "recalled experience of death," and ~3% reported overt visual/auditory awareness. It also recorded EEG markers (gamma, delta, theta, alpha) emerging during CPR in some patients. [Parnia2023]

## What is NOT established `[OPEN]`

**The cause.** Multiple plausible mechanisms compete, none confirmed as *the* explanation, and they are not mutually exclusive:

- **Cerebral hypoxia/anoxia** and the dysfunction it causes
- The **end-of-life gamma surge** (see [`../facts/brain-at-death.md`](../facts/brain-at-death.md)) as a possible neural substrate
- **REM-intrusion** / arousal-system phenomena
- **Endogenous neurochemistry** (proposed roles for DMT, ketamine-like NMDA effects, endorphins — all `[SPECULATIVE]` as specific NDE causes)
- **Expectation, memory reconstruction, and narrative** shaping the recalled account after the fact

**Veridical perception** — the strong claim. The scientifically decisive test would be a survivor accurately reporting a *specific, verifiable* fact they could not have known except by perceiving during the period of arrest (e.g., a hidden visual target). AWARE and AWARE-II were explicitly designed to test this with placed visual targets. The result: **no confirmed hits** on the targets — the strong veridical-perception claim remains **unconfirmed**. Anecdotal accounts of accurate perception exist but resist rigorous verification. [Parnia2023]

## Why it stays `[OPEN]`

- The brain states during cardiac arrest are extreme, brief, and nearly impossible to instrument cleanly (AWARE-II got interpretable EEG from only 53 of 567 patients).
- Reports are necessarily *retrospective* — collected after recovery — so memory reconstruction can't be excluded.
- The phenomenology is consistent enough to demand explanation but underdetermines *which* mechanism produces it.

## The disciplined position

- ❌ "NDEs prove consciousness survives the body / prove an afterlife." — The decisive veridical-perception test has not produced confirmed results.
- ❌ "NDEs are just hallucinations, fully explained." — No single mechanism is confirmed; "fully explained" overstates the science.
- ✅ "NDEs are robustly documented experiences with several competing, unconfirmed neurobiological explanations. Whether they reflect veridical perception during arrest has been directly tested and not confirmed. The phenomenon is real; its interpretation is open."
