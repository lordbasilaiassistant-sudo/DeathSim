# DeathSim

**An honest, source-backed catalog of what happens to a human being when they die — rigorously separating what is *known* from what is *unknown* from what is merely *theorized*.**

Almost everything written about death conflates three different things:

1. **Settled biology** — measurable, reproducible, not seriously disputed.
2. **Genuine unknowns** — questions science has *tried* to answer and cannot yet resolve.
3. **Theories** — frameworks and speculation, ranging from the scientific-default to the fringe.

This repository refuses to blur them. Every claim carries a **confidence tag** (see [`TAXONOMY.md`](./TAXONOMY.md)) and, where it asserts a fact, a citation. If something is speculation, it says so. If we don't know, we say "we don't know" — and we say *why* we can't know it yet.

This is not a comfort document and not a debunking document. It is a ledger.

## How to read this

- **[`facts/`](./facts/)** — the established physiology and timeline of death. High-confidence, cited.
- **[`unknowns/`](./unknowns/)** — questions with real experiments behind them and no settled answer (consciousness at death, near-death experiences).
- **[`theories/`](./theories/)** — explicitly labeled frameworks, from the scientific default to the speculative fringe. Nothing here is presented as fact.
- **[`SOURCES.md`](./SOURCES.md)** — master citation list.
- **[`TAXONOMY.md`](./TAXONOMY.md)** — the confidence tags and the rules that govern every entry.

## The one rule

> **Cite the evidence, or mark it as a hypothesis. No exceptions.**

A claim with no source and no `[SPECULATIVE]` / `[OPEN]` tag is a bug. Open an issue.

## Status

Early seed. The biology of bodily death is well-mapped; the question of *experience* at and after death is where the honest unknowns live, and that boundary is the entire point of this project.
